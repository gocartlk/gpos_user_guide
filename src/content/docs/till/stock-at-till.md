:::note[Depends on your plan]
**Stock view at the till** is a plan feature, part of **Stock control**. Without it, the till has
no **Stock** button and search results do not show the quantity. See
[Plans and features](/getting-started/plans/).
:::

Cashiers can answer *"Do you have any more of these?"* without leaving the counter.

## In the search results

When you search or scan, each result shows **In stock** with the branch's quantity. It is coloured
when the item is **low** (at or below its reorder level) or **out of stock**.

## The Stock screen

Select <span class="ui">Stock</span> at the top of the till (**Ctrl + I**). **Stock in this branch**
lists the items with their quantity.

- **Scan a barcode or type a name or SKU** to find an item.
- **Show**: **All**, **Low stock**, **Out of stock**, **Expiring** (within **30**, **60** or **90
  days**) or **Expired**. The expiry filters show each **batch** with *Expires in … days*,
  *Expires today* or *Expired … days ago* — they need the **Batches and expiry dates** feature.
- Select <span class="ui">Show more</span> for the next items.

The till shows quantities only — **never cost or stock value**.

## Offline

Stock is read from GPOS while you are online. Offline the till says *Stock is not available
offline.*

With a [local hub](/business/local-hub/), the till still shows stock when the shop's internet is
down: *No internet: stock as of 10:15, with every till's sales since.* Stock received or adjusted
in the back office shows once the internet is back.
